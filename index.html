Smartphone Micro-Nephelometer

Led a 4-member team to build a demo prototype of a smartphone-based nephelometer for point-of-care urine turbidity screening. It uses 90-degree light scattering with a 635 nm LED, a sealed micro-volume cartridge, a custom 3D-printed optical housing designed in Fusion360, and a Python/OpenCV image processing pipeline calibrated against standard turbidity suspensions.
